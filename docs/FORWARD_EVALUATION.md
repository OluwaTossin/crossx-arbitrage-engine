# Minimum 14-day forward evaluation protocol — NOT started

This is a separate milestone requiring human review, operating capacity and actual elapsed observation. Do not infer completion from unit tests, a short smoke run or replay. Do not start automatically.

1. Freeze the reviewed commit, runtime image ids, scenario config hashes, instrument availability responses and fee/rule evidence. Record explicit GBP/USD assumption date and value. Create six scenarios: GBP 500/1000/1500, equal/selective allocation. Do not share simulated cash across scenarios or duplicate it within a scenario.
2. Use a dedicated collection database and isolated Compose project. Forecast disk from measured bytes/observation and ingress. Prior Phase 1 measurements were about 15–16GB/day for two venues; fourteen days can exceed 200GB before additional feeds, indexes, WAL, monitoring and archives. Measure four-venue growth first. Confirm free capacity with headroom or approve a documented sampling/archival plan. Default budgets are warnings, not quotas.
3. Verify all eight public spot feeds, exact USD market identity, checksum/sequence integrity, reconnect generations, clock age, queue residence, batching, persistence and storage alerts. Public API availability is independent of UK trading eligibility. Start only public ingestion; exchange accounts are unnecessary.
4. Explicitly invoke bounded `crossx.forward` sessions per scenario, with documented commit lag and a dedicated single ingestion writer. A stopped or failed session remains incomplete, never spliced invisibly into a continuous result. If long sessions restart, preserve every session and quantify missing intervals; do not reset capital and add results as though capital were unchanged.
5. Review at least daily: observed hours and per-pair coverage, missing/stale frames, sequence/CRC failures, reconnect durations, queue pressure, DB failures, CPU/memory, actual disk growth and free capacity; raw/theoretical/capital-feasible/executed counts; reasons for rejection; realised, unrealised and total P&L; fees and slippage; max drawdown; failed-leg unhedged exposure; inventory depletion and estimated rebalance costs. Review every artificial fault scenario separately from market-derived outcomes.
6. Test optional local Alertmanager routing before use; inspect delivered webhook messages in alert-sink logs. No external email/Slack/on-call integration is assumed. Ensure a human watches local alerts. Stop new collection on unsafe disk pressure without deleting evidence. Restart only the dedicated environment, and record outage boundaries and image identities.
7. Export closed receipt intervals with `crossx.archive`, verify checksum/schema/count/order, preserve source reports and replay all scenarios from the same closed data. Document missing generation markers and limited-depth uncertainty. An archive is not a backup until its independent copy has been verified.
8. Go/no-go criteria: at least 14 real elapsed days with complete duration accounting; all required pairs either observed or explicitly unavailable; no silently bridged sequence gaps; no stale book causing a simulated fill; no negative unreserved cash/base balances; consistent capital conservation and ledger reconciliation; reproducible event/report hashes for closed replay; every failed leg and unhedged quantity reconciled; no unexplained DB or restart loss; tested notification route and sufficient disk headroom; all security findings triaged. Profit is not an acceptance target. A negative result is valid research.

Go/no-go review may reject the research methodology even if the software is working. The present simulator does not prove queue position, actual order execution, transfer feasibility, regulatory eligibility or future profitability. Any unresolved source-data completeness issue makes related execution estimates unevaluable. Live trading remains outside scope.
